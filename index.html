CE4CSID Working Group

Community engagement, built into CSID research from the start.

Climate Sensitive Infectious Disease research is often run at a high scientific level, without the communities who face its direct impact. This toolkit gives researchers practical resources and guidance to change that — from project design through to evaluation. Free and open access, always.

Why this exists

Research shapes communities. Communities should shape research.

Involving communities from the earliest stages of project design helps shape interventions in ways that benefit both CSID professionals and the people who face the impact directly — whether the initiative involves research, policy, or the development of digital tools.

Better science

Communities often see risks, patterns, and practical barriers that a purely scientific lens misses. Engagement closes that gap early, when it is cheapest to act on.

Stronger trust

CSID research often returns to the same communities over time. Genuine engagement protects that relationship for the studies that come after this one.

Fewer blind spots

Interventions designed without community input tend to underperform in the field. Early engagement surfaces the constraints that matter before they cause failure.

How the toolkit is organised

Four resource families. One interactive checklist.

The toolkit groups its resources by how you will actually use them: Planning Guidance, Engagement Frameworks, Case Examples, and Templates. Alongside those documents sits an interactive Community Engagement Checklist that walks a research team through five planning stages.
