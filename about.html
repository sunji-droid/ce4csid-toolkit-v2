About the toolkit

Who we are, and who this toolkit is built for.

The CE4CSID Working Group brings together researchers and practitioners working on Climate Sensitive Infectious Disease, with a shared view that community involvement should shape a project from its earliest stages — not just its final reporting.

The working group

Practical guidance, grounded in real CSID work.

The CE4CSID Working Group has been developing a set of practical resources and guidelines that researchers can use to plan and integrate community engagement into their CSID research programmes. Climate Sensitive Infectious Disease sits at the intersection of environmental change and health, and its impact is felt first and most directly by the communities living with that change.

While numerous CSID initiatives exist globally, many are run at a high scientific level and do not include the communities who face the direct impact of CSID. This toolkit exists to close that gap — with resources that are practical enough to use on a real project timeline and budget.

In short: involving communities from the earliest stages of project design helps shape interventions in ways that benefit both CSID professionals and community members — whether the initiative involves research, policy, or the development of digital tools.
Who this toolkit is for

Built for the people planning and running CSID work.

Researchers

Designing and running CSID studies who want engagement built into the methodology, not bolted on afterwards.

Programme implementers

Turning research findings into interventions, tools, or services that communities are expected to adopt and trust.

Policy makers

Shaping CSID related policy who need a credible record of community perspective behind their decisions.

How to use this site

Start wherever your project is right now.

Whether you are at the earliest design stage or already in the field, the toolkit meets you where you are.

  1. Read the Toolkit Resources

    Start with Planning Guidance if you are still shaping the project, or jump to the category that matches your current stage.

  2. Download what your team needs

    Every resource and template is available on the Downloads page, ready to adapt to your specific study.

  3. Work through the Checklist

    Use the interactive checklist to track engagement actions stage by stage, with notes and a printable export for your project file.

  4. Reach out with questions

    Contact BSHD and the CE4CSID Working Group directly if you need guidance specific to your project context.
