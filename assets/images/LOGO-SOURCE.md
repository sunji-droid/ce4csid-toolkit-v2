# CSID Network logo assets

The logo files in this directory are the official CSID Network artwork supplied with the
**CSID Network Identity Guidelines** (last updated 8 October 2025). They were taken from the
logo pack linked by the guide's "Download" button on the Logo Variations page, not redrawn.

Used here:

- `logo.svg`: primary full lockup, brand teal (header).
- `logo-white.svg`: full lockup, neutral white (footer / dark backgrounds).
- `favicon.svg`: stand-alone globe logomark, brand teal.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`: official social-media avatar (blue circle with margins).
- `csid-logo-mix.png`, `csid-logo-teal.png`: raster copies used for share images and documents.

Brand rule: do not recolour the globe icon with other brand colours. Use the logo in full
colour, brand teal, black, or white only, and keep clear space around the mark.
