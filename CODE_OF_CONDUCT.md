# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want the CE4CSID Community Engagement Toolkit to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or location.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback gracefully.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep discussion focused on the toolkit and its community-engagement purpose.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or contribution.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer at **mkonamile@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.
